Services

Concept to prototype

If you have a rough idea and need to see it before committing real resources, I use diagrams, wireframes or AI-assisted prototyping to turn it into something you can react to fast.

Product design & UX

If your product feels confusing or inconsistent to use, I design the flows and interfaces that make the experience make sense.

Product analytics & insights

If you have data but can't tell what it's actually telling you, I build the metrics and reporting that show what's working and what isn't.

Product workflows & systems

If your team ships without a repeatable process and every release feels like a surprise, I build the workflow and rituals that make delivery predictable.

Approach

01

Identify what's not working

I run an audit of the product and any existing feedback you have on what needs attention. I map the flows, actions, and issues so we can discuss prioritization and direction.

02

Explore and prototype

With a shared direction, I start working on how to solve the problem. From user flows to vibe-coded prototypes, I explore the best direction and bring it back for review.

03

Build, test, refine, repeat

With approval, I work with your team to get it built, make sure we have the data points to test, and analyze what's working to tweak what's necessary in the next iteration.
